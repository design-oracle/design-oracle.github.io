1. Dream

Let your mind drift beyond the boundaries of what is, into infinite space, where innovation begins. Here, in the realm of pure potential, the seeds of new experiences take root.

Ritual

  1. Find a quiet space with a blank notebook.
  2. Start a timer for 15 minutes.
  3. Begin by writing "In my wildest dreams, this project would..."
  4. Let your mind wander beyond constraints of time, technology, or feasibility.

Oracle

Upright. Your imagination needs room to soar. Release the constraints of "how" and focus on "what if." This is a time for blue-sky thinking and radical possibilities. Trust that practical solutions can emerge from seemingly impossible dreams. Your subconscious is trying to tell you something – listen to those wild ideas you usually dismiss.

Inverted. You might be stuck in analysis paralysis or too focused on limitations. Are you letting past failures or current constraints clip your creative wings? Alternatively, you might be lost in dreams without grounding them in reality. Find the balance between imagination and actionable ideas.
